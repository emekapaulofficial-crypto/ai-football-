    How PredictIQ works

    1

    Enter the teams

    Type the home and away team names. Screenshot upload is optional.

    2

    Verify recent form

    The system retrieves completed recent matches and checks data quality before modelling.

    3

    Run the ensemble

    Form, team strength, attack, defence and a Dixon-Coles corrected goal model are combined and calibrated.

    4

    Explain the prediction

    The report shows probabilities, expected goals, Over/Under, BTTS, correct scores, key reasons and risk factors.
