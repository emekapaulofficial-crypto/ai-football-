    How PredictIQ works

    1

    Enter teams

    Type the actual home and away team names. For youth matches, include “U21” so the correct youth team is researched.

    2

    Research both teams

    The system retrieves completed results from public football data and selects the latest five verified matches.

    3

    Compare form

    Recent goals, results, attack, defence and recency weighting are used for both sides.

    4

    Predict the coming match

    The ensemble produces the home, draw and away probabilities and explains the evidence.
