    03

    How PredictIQ works

    1

    Enter the teams

    Type the home and away team names. Screenshot upload is optional.

    2

    Fetch the last five

    The system retrieves completed recent matches from public football data and checks that both teams have enough verified results.

    3

    Build team profiles

    Recent goals, goals conceded, clean sheets, scoring consistency and results are converted into attack, defence and form scores.

    4

    Predict the outcome

    A multi-signal model estimates expected goals, Home/Draw/Away probabilities, Over/Under, BTTS, Double Chance and exact-score probabilities.
