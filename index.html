    03

    How PredictIQ works

    1

    Read the screenshot

    OCR extracts team names and decimal odds. The parser normalizes common markets such as 1X2, Over/Under, Double Chance and BTTS.

    2

    Build team profiles

    Recent results are used to estimate attack, defence, scoring consistency and home/away context. The latest match receives more weight.

    3

    Run an independent model

    A goal-distribution model estimates expected goals and score probabilities, then derives market probabilities. The bookmaker odds are used for comparison, not copied as the prediction.

    4

    Rank — or reject

    Every readable market is scored for probability, implied probability, edge and data quality. If nothing qualifies, PredictIQ can return “No qualifying option”.
